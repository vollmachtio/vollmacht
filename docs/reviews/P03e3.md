## Retained limitations

The driver trusts fixture enrollment on stdin and must not be exposed to agents. The challenge construction is experimental, not a canonical Human Mandate. A compromised helper can forge a success claim; its code, dependency tree and runtime remain trusted. There is no durable registry/replay store, production packaging integrity, agent proof or execution authorization. Tests are evidence against covered failures, not a guarantee of absence of bugs or vulnerabilities.
