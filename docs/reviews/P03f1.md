Independent reviewer: `adversarial_p02`. Initial review found a P2 cleanup bug: an exited group leader caused cleanup to skip its surviving descendants. The fix retains the leader PID with `waitid(WNOWAIT)`, signals the group before reaping, and attempts leader cleanup and pipe closure even when group cleanup fails. Escaping process groups is outside this trusted developer-tool boundary.

Reports and documentation now state that doctor validates executable files but does not prove launcher/driver function or architecture. Measurement exercises the actual integration. Hashes and package metadata are observations, not authenticated artifact provenance or installed-content verification.
